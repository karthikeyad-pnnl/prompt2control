INFORMATIVE APPENDIX C 
ADDENDA DESCRIPTION 
ASHRAE Guideline 36-2024 incorporates ASHRAE Guideline 36-2021 and Addenda a, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, j, l, m, n, o, p, q, r, 
s, t, u, v, w, y and z to ASHRAE Guideline 36-2021. Table C-1 lists each addendum and describes the way in which the 
standard is affected by the change. It also lists the ASHRAE approval dates for each addendum.
